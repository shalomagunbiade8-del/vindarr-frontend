ABOUT VINDARR

A new way to experience African commerce.

Vindarr is a video-first commerce platform designed around the way modern consumers discover products, stories and businesses.

01

Video-first commerce

Instead of asking shoppers to search through endless product listings, Vindarr puts discovery first. Businesses and creators can use video to show products, explain value, demonstrate use and tell stories.

02

Digitalizing the Afro shopping experience

Vindarr is built to help informal and emerging African businesses become more discoverable, build trust and reach customers beyond their immediate networks.

03

More than products

Users can discover and purchase ebooks, fashion products and everyday essentials — creating one ecosystem for commerce, knowledge and culture.

04

Shoppertainment

Vindarr combines entertainment and shopping so that discovery itself becomes part of the experience.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Commerce is becoming more experiential.

The opportunity is particularly significant across Africa, where mobile technology and social commerce are changing how businesses reach consumers.

A VINDARR PRODUCT INSIGHT

People want more than a product listing.

In commerce environments where trust, context and social proof matter, richer product discovery can help shoppers make better-informed decisions. Vindarr therefore combines video, visual context, demonstrations, conversations, creator stories and community signals — giving consumers more ways to understand what they are considering before they buy.
